Architecture

Our heaviest discipline. Engineered to build.

Architecture is the foundation every project stands on. Most interior designers stop at the mood board — we prepare coordinated, permit-ready architectural plan sets in-house, then carry that same documentation through procurement, construction, and install. It is the most demanding part of what we do, and the part every other discipline here is built around.

  • ◆ As-built field measurement & existing-condition documentation
  • ◆ Site & setback plans with dimensioned property clearances
  • ◆ Existing & proposed floor, roof & elevation plans
  • ◆ Dimensioned cabinetry, appliance & fixture schedules
  • ◆ Structural engineer coordination & city plan-check submittals
  • ◆ Remodel & construction management through completion
  • ◆ Furniture, fixtures & décor sourced, delivered & placed
