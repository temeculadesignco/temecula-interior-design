Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Temecula Interior Design (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit temeculainteriordesign.com, call our phone line or receive text messages from us.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address and project address.
  • Your phone number, the line you called and the time of the call when you call us.
  • Voicemail messages you choose to leave, including recordings and transcripts.
  • Details you share about your project when you fill out an estimate or contact form.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited and browser type.

How we use your information

  • To answer your inquiry, call you back and prepare estimates and proposals.
  • To send the text messages you request, such as the link to our free estimate form, replies about your inquiry or estimate, and appointment follow-ups.
  • To run, secure and improve our website and phone system.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

Text messaging and mobile information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared, sold, rented or traded to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information and text messaging consent are used only to deliver the messages you asked for and to respond to you. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties.

We use service providers, such as our phone and text messaging carrier, only to deliver our own messages and calls to you. They are not allowed to use your information for their own marketing.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our Text Messaging page and Terms for full program details.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (for example phone, email, hosting and payment services), when you ask us to, or when the law requires it. The text messaging exclusion above applies to all sharing.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and project records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and tax obligations, then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, delete and correct personal information and the right not to be discriminated against for using these rights. To make a request, email admin@temeculainteriordesign.com.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
