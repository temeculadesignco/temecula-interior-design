From Concept to Permit: Anatomy of a Buildable Set

By Temecula Interior Design  ·  June 2026  ·  2 min read

A beautiful idea becomes a home only when it can be drawn, stamped, and approved.

There is a meaningful gap between a design that looks good and a design that can be built — the work most companies never touch. A permit-ready set starts with accurate as-built measurements taken on site, then carries existing and proposed floor, roof, and elevation plans, a dimensioned site/setback plan, and integrated structural detail through plan-check and into construction without losing its integrity.

Decorators stop at the mood board. Buildable design continues all the way to the stamp.

Plan-check is a dialogue with the city, and the specifics of what a reviewer will and won't accept vary by jurisdiction and project. If you have a site in mind, we can tell you plainly what the path to permit looks like.
