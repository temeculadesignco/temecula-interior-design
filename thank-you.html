What happens next

  1. Intake call. We’ll reach out within one business day to book your intake call.
  2. Site visit. We measure and photograph your home, kitchens and bathrooms included.
  3. Your design. A full modern minimalist design for your home up to 3,000 sq ft, with up to 3 revisions.
  4. In-person presentation. Michael or one of our designers presents your design in person and hand delivers everything to you.
  5. Fulfillment, only if you want it. Purchasing, furnishings and installation are quoted and invoiced separately, after the presentation.
