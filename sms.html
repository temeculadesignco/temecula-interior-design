Text Messaging

Text messaging
opt-in.

Last updated October 26, 2026

Temecula Interior Design sends text messages only to people who ask for them by pressing 1 in our phone menu.

How to opt in

When you call the Temecula Interior Design line at (951) 718-7778, our automated phone menu plays this message:

“Thank you for calling Temecula Interior Design. We're so glad you reached out. For a text with the link to our free estimate form, press 1. To have our team call you back, press 2. To leave a message, press 3.”

Pressing 1 is your request and consent to receive text messages from Temecula Interior Design at the mobile number you are calling from. We do not text you unless you press 1. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase. If you press 2 or 3, or simply stay on the line, you will not receive texts from us.

What you will receive

Customer care texts about your inquiry: the link to our free estimate form that you asked for, replies about your inquiry or estimate, and follow-ups about appointments you schedule with us. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

Example: “Thanks for calling Temecula Interior Design! Here is the link to our free estimate form: https://www.temeculadesigntech.com/#contact Reply STOP to opt out.”

  • Program name: Temecula Interior Design Customer Care Texts
  • Message frequency varies.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one confirmation text and no further messages. You can also reply CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE or STOPALL. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP or INFO for help, or email admin@temeculainteriordesign.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We never share or sell your mobile number or text messaging opt-in consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Read our Privacy Policy and our Terms and Text Messaging Terms.
